1.2 Training, Tuning & Prompts Paradigm

No weights were modified or trained from scratch. The system utilizes zero-shot and few-shot structural role prompts with strict system instructions and forced JSON schemas. Prompts enforce strict clinical boundaries: blocking diagnostic claims, preventing drug dosage recommendations, and barring the LLM from setting or modifying patient risk ratings.

3. Limitations & Ethical Safeguards

3.1 System Limitations

  • OCR Dependency: Gemini Vision OCR accuracy is highly dependent on image quality, low-light exposures, and handwriting legibility. Unread documents are flagged for human inspection.
  • Dialect Variation: Bangla symptom extraction can vary due to regional dialects (e.g., Chittagonian, Sylheti). The system prompts are optimized for colloquial Bangla but may miss highly localized descriptors.
  • Offline Connectivity: Zero-shot LLM queries require active internet connections. Local cache guidelines and deterministic checklists act as offline fallbacks.

3.2 Safety Decoupling & Privacy Safeguards

  • Separation of Powers: Generative AI models handle linguistic translation and OCR extraction only. Risk levels (LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH) are computed by a local deterministic rules validator (json-rules-engine).
  • Output Safety Validator: A regex-based post-process filter checks LLM outputs. It blocks prescription names, dosage descriptions, and diagnostic claims, replacing them with standard warning templates.
  • Consent-Gated Data Sharing: Patients retain total control. Health workers cannot view clinical trend history or uploaded documents unless the mother sets consentToShareWithHealthWorker to true.
