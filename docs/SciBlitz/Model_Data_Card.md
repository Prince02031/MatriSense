### 1. Model Details

#### 1.1 Pre-trained Models Used
*   **LLM Provider & Model:** Google Gemini 1.5 Flash (API-based)
    *   **Provider:** Google AI
    *   **License:** Custom/Commercial (Permitted for competition/prototype use)
    *   **Role in System:** Parses informal, colloquial Bangla symptoms into standardized English symptom arrays; generates natural Bangla explanations grounded in retrieved care cards; generates follow-up screening questions.
*   **Embedding Model:** Xenova/multilingual-e5-small (Local inference via Transformers.js in Node.js)
    *   **Provider:** Hugging Face / Microsoft
    *   **License:** MIT License
    *   **Role in System:** Generates semantic embeddings for maternal health guideline chunks and care-guidance cards to enable local vector search.
*   **Offline LLM Option:** Qwen 2.5 (7B) (Local inference via Ollama)
    *   **Provider:** Alibaba Group / Community
    *   **License:** Apache 2.0 License
    *   **Role in System:** Serves as a local, offline LLM fallback option for symptom translation and summarization when cloud APIs are disconnected.

#### 1.2 Training & Customization
No models were trained from scratch. The system uses a zero-shot/few-shot role prompting paradigm with strict JSON schema outputs. System prompts enforce strict clinical boundaries:
*   No medical diagnostic terminology.
*   No medication prescription or dosage advice.
*   Zero risk level modifications (the LLM cannot override the deterministic rule engine).

### 2. Data Details

#### 2.1 Datasets & Knowledge Sources
MatriSense utilizes a hybrid knowledge repository built on top of validated public health sources:
*   **Source 1: WHO Antenatal Care Guidelines**
    *   **Origin:** World Health Organization (WHO)
    *   **Content:** Maternal danger signs, routine pregnancy timelines, and risk factors.
*   **Source 2: CDC HEAR HER Campaign**
    *   **Origin:** US Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC)
    *   **Content:** Urgent maternal warning signs, symptom checklists, and patient advocacy instructions.
*   **Source 3: NHS Pregnancy Guide & UK Maternal Health Cards**
    *   **Origin:** National Health Service (NHS), UK
    *   **Content:** Safe self-care protocols for low-risk common pregnancy complaints (e.g., morning sickness, mild backache).
*   **Source 4: Synthetic Evaluation Dataset**
    *   **Origin:** Manually generated by the development team (25 cases)
    *   **Content:** Simulated Bangla symptom scripts representing various risk categories (low, medium, high) for system testing and validation.

#### 2.2 Ingestion & Processing
*   **Formats:** JSON, Markdown, HTML, PDF.
*   **Symptom Mapping:** Bangla synonyms are mapped to normalized codes (e.g., `রক্তপাত` -> `vaginal_bleeding`).
*   **Data Lineage:** Every retrieved care card retains strict metadata referencing its original source (e.g., "WHO-ANC-2016-p34").

### 3. Limitations & Ethical Concerns

#### 3.1 Known Limitations
1. **Dialect and Idiomatic Extraction:** The model's ability to extract symptoms is highly dependent on Gemini's understanding of regional dialects (e.g., Sylheti or Chittagonian). If the model fails to extract a symptom, the raw input is flagged for human review.
2. **False Reassurance Risk:** Generative output might sound authoritative and reassure a mother incorrectly. To prevent this, the Explainer is prohibited from using words like "safe," "nothing to worry about," or "normal," and is forced to display warning signs.
3. **No Offline Support for LLM:** The API-based LLM component requires internet connectivity. In offline settings, the app falls back to a deterministic checkbox-based symptom questionnaire and local cached guidelines.

#### 3.2 Ethical & Safety Guardrails
*   **Human-in-the-Loop:** All automated triage results and referral preferences must be reviewed and accepted by a registered community health worker or doctor.
*   **The Triage Split:** The generative model is physically decoupled from the triage decision. Risk levels (LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH) are decided strictly by a deterministic rule engine (`json-rules-engine`) and cannot be manipulated by generative model hallucinations.
*   **Censorship of Medication:** A post-generation regex validator blocks any mention of pharmaceuticals (e.g., "Paracetamol", "Napa", "Antacid") or diagnostic assertions (e.g., "You have pre-eclampsia").
